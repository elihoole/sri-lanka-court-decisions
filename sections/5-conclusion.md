# **Conclustion**

In this paper, we introduced CDTextLK -- a text corpus containing 1278 Supreme Court decisions and 7314 Court of Appeal decisions from Sri Lanka. We also presented some descriptive statistics on the performance of the country’s apex courts, identified most salient words in the corpus, and modelled most common topics and their word distributions. As mentioned at the beginning, the primitive stage of digitalisation of Sri Lanka’s legal system necessarily entails that the work of this paper, although a significant contribution in the compilation and sharing of the decision text corpus and initial insights, is also preliminary in nature. The computational methods used even at this stage, however, have yielded findings demonstrative of the functioning of the superior courts of Sri Lanka –- often exposing or confirming trends within the justice system that merit further investigation, and in some cases, urgent solutions. While the immediate next steps in relation to the corpus and computational analysis are clear and have been detailed above, they serve then not just to explore other features of the decision making process in the Superior Courts, but also to further build on and explore some of the findings already highlighted in this paper.
